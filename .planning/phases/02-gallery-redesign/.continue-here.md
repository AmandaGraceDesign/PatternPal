<decisions_made>

- Photo-based mockups should zero out procedural perspective/displacement when using real photos — the photo already has natural perspective and texture
- Multiply blend washes out dark/saturated patterns on bright warm photos — source-over + lighting soft-light preserves color better
- Displacement maps from Photoshop (desaturated + blurred + contrast-boosted photo) give more realistic fold distortion than procedural noise
- UV-map approach is sound but needs higher precision than 8-bit RGB channels (256 positions across 3360px source = 13px bands). Could work with 16-bit maps or dithering in a future attempt
- Mask bounds should always be scanned with Pillow, not guessed visually
</decisions_made>
