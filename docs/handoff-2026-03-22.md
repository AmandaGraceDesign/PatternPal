## What still needs work

### Priority 1: Pattern scaling
- The pipeline uses `patternArea.width / (physicalSize.width * dpi)` for tile scaling, but `physicalSize` is a single global value
- Each zone may need its own physical width for correct scale
- Current `physicalSize: { width: 14, height: 22 }` is approximate — real kids dress (size 4-6) is roughly 12" chest, 22" total length
- The scale calculation needs rethinking for image-based mockups where the photo isn't at a known DPI

### Priority 2: Mask alignment
- Zone `patternArea` coordinates (x, y, width, height) are rough estimates based on visual inspection of the masks
- Need to verify these align with the actual white areas in the mask images
- Consider auto-detecting mask bounds (like V1's `getMaskBounds`) to set `patternArea` automatically

### Priority 3: Displacement tuning
- Reduced displacement intensity significantly for photo-based mockups
- May want to disable procedural displacement entirely for image-based templates and rely on the photo's natural texture via multiply blend
- Or: extract displacement from the actual product photo luminance instead of generating procedurally

### Priority 4: Perspective
- Reduced perspective squeeze but may need further tuning per-zone
- The bodice should have minimal perspective (flat, fitted)
- The skirt should have slight A-line flare perspective
